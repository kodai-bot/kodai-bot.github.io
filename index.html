Dylan Foley

building computational systems for archaeology

Archaeologist and AI/data scientist. About fifteen years of field archaeology, an MSc in AI & Big Data, and a research programme that treats archaeological evidence as signals of physical processes.

The loop

trace → recognise structure → generate candidate process → forward-simulate → predict trace → compare → revise
