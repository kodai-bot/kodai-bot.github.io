Streedagh, 1588 · the signal record

From trace to ship

Everything said about the three Armada wrecks at Streedagh starts as a trace someone looked at: a gun lifted by divers, a spread of magnetometer readings, a line in a survivor's letter. Pick one and follow it: from the act of looking, through the documents that carry it to us, to what is inferred from it. Each step says how it is known.

How to read a chain

source
What was seen or written: the act of looking, the words as printed, a figure as stated. Kept apart from everything below it.
derived
Arithmetic on a stated figure, with its recipe: a weight mark in cantara turned into kilograms, codos into metres.
inferred
A judgement with a probability and a falsifier: which ship a gun belongs to, when an event happened, what a passage records.
hypothesis
Stated before the evidence that would test it, with what would prove it wrong. Untested until that evidence arrives.
